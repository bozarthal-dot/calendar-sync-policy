Privacy Policy

Bozarth Family Calendar Sync · Effective 2026-09-12

Bozarth Family Calendar Sync is a personal, single-user utility that mirrors the owner's own Apple calendar into a Google Calendar the owner also controls.

It reads and writes only that owner's calendar data, using Google Calendar API access the owner grants to it directly. No data is collected, stored outside the owner's own devices and Google account, shared, or sold to any third party. No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind is used.

The application is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.
