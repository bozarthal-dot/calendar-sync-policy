Privacy Policy

Bozarth Drive Backup · Effective 2026-10-31

Bozarth Drive Backup is a personal, single-user utility that copies the owner's own Google Drive to a backup disk the owner also controls.

It reads only that owner's Drive data, using read-only Google Drive API access the owner grants to it directly, and writes nothing to Drive. No data is collected, stored anywhere other than the owner's own devices, shared, or sold to any third party. No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind is used.

The application is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.
